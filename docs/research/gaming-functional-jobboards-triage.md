# Gaming and functional-language feed triage

Reviewed November 2, 2026. These names are candidates, not additional production
sources. No provider contact, accounts, payments or writes were performed.

| Candidate | Primary evidence | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remote Game Jobs | [Terms](https://remotegamejobs.com/terms) limit content copying to personal, noncommercial use. [Discord guidance](https://remotegamejobs.com/discord) supports following announcement channels into communities. | Discord permission does not establish commercial full-description RSS/API republication permission. Hold independent commercial aggregation pending a specific supported grant. |
| Work With Indies | [Terms](https://www.workwithindies.com/terms), updated November 24, 2025, prohibit crawling/scraping in Prohibited Uses. General material access license does not clearly authorize full-text redistribution. | No qualifying publisher feed grant found. Do not build HTML scraping around this restriction. |
| ElixirJobs.net | [Home](https://elixirjobs.net/) explicitly links [RSS](https://elixirjobs.net/rss). [Terms §4.1](https://elixirjobs.net/terms-and-conditions) require express written permission to copy/distribute/disclose accessible content. | RSS exists, but commercial aggregation scope is blocked by the express reuse restriction. No full-feed probe needed. |
| Hubstaff Talent | [Original host](https://talent.hubstaff.com/) redirects to [hubstafftalent.net](https://hubstafftalent.net/). [Terms §§2–3,7](https://hubstaff.com/terms) cover Talent, reserve service rights and grant submitted-content publication rights to Hubstaff. | No official public RSS endpoint or publisher grant found in the reviewed official pages/search. Free employer/job-seeker service does not prove free data republication. Unresolved, not a demonstrated blanket ban on every integration. |
| Remote4Me | [Homepage](https://remote4me.com/) describes an aggregator; category links presently lead to a shared Google document. | No documented public feed/full-text/date contract or redistribution grant found. Do not count its claimed input boards as independent integrations. |
| HexHire | [Founder announcement](https://elixirforum.com/t/built-hexhire-io-a-centralized-feed-for-all-elixir-jobs/73595) confirms an RSS feed added for community readers. [Official site](https://hexhire.io/) publishes aggregated Elixir roles. | Reader RSS availability alone does not establish full-text publisher reuse. No explicit aggregation grant verified yet; no adapter proposed. |
| Brave Clojure Jobs | [Official board](https://jobs.braveclojure.com/) shows postings; latest visible listing was August 14, 2026. | No useful fresh sample or explicit redistribution/feed contract established. Low priority, not a prohibited-source conclusion. |
| Rails Job Board / JobKit | [JobKit](https://www.jobkit.io/) explicitly supports RSS aggregators; [Rails Foundation](https://rubyonrails.org/2023/11/27/rails-job-board-is-live) identifies the platform and feed. | Implemented experimental collector with full text, original dates and seven US-eligible sample listings. See [Rails research](./rails-jobs.md). Count JobKit once. |

This pass produced one implementable source. Candidates with incomplete evidence
remain research items; they do not inflate source readiness or production counts.
