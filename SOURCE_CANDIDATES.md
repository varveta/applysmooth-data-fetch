# ApplySmooth: 38 additional platform candidates

Research date: 2026-10-30. Scope: add **38 distinct platforms** to existing 12 (Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Personio, Recruitee, Jobicy, Himalayas, We Work Remotely, RemoteOK, Remotive). Multiple employers on one ATS remain one platform.

**Finding:** 50 distinct supported production integrations cannot be declared ready from current evidence. Six candidates have documented public job surfaces; employer selection, permission, US relevance, freshness, adapter behavior, and local/TEST validation remain separate gates. Most remaining candidates require employer credentials, feed provisioning, publisher approval, commercial terms, or an aggregation export not yet established. This inventory is a roadmap, not 38 implemented integrations.

## Evidence labels

- **Public candidate:** official documentation describes a public listing/feed route; not a blanket commercial redistribution grant.
- **Provisioning:** employer-provided key/feed or provider registration required. Do not reuse another party's private account credentials.
- **Agreement:** publisher/commercial/partner arrangement required or commercial reuse unclear.
- **Route gap:** official material reviewed does not establish a suitable backend ingestion export. This means unverified, not proof that no arrangement exists.
- **US potential:** employer-scoped ATS rows can contain US jobs, but each selected employer must be tested; the table makes no unmeasured coverage claim.

## Delivery sequence and decision tradeoffs

1. Finish Pinpoint backend and isolated preview acceptance after the local TEST adapter validation. Evaluate Manatal and Trakstar on real authorized US employers; TalentLyft needs a nonempty board. Keep these in dry-run/observation-only TEST until freshness and verification pass.
2. Provision USAJOBS, then employer-published JobScore/Comeet/Homerun/Paylocity feeds and relevant public Bullhorn tenants. Keys alone do not resolve republication conditions.
3. Evaluate paid aggregators only after measuring deduplicated accepted US jobs from direct employers. Adzuna, Jooble and Careerjet may add reach but also overlap, fees, attribution and freshness delays.
4. Leave route-gap candidates off ingestion workflows. Inventory entries do not count as integrated sources. A disabled workflow returning success without fetching is not validation.

Benefits: broader employer/industry coverage, less dependence on one provider, more direct application URLs, and possible US federal/hourly/staffing coverage beyond remote-tech boards.

Costs: duplicate listings across aggregators and ATS sources; employer discovery/onboarding; 50 independent rate budgets and failure modes; credentials and commercial agreements; conflicting location and remote eligibility; incomplete posting dates; additional verifier/closure logic and Actions runtime. More platforms do not necessarily produce more *accepted unique US jobs*.

Promotion evidence should include per-source fetched/normalized/US-eligible/fresh/quarantined/deduplicated/accepted counts, complete pagination, stale and expired job handling, canonical identity, bounded retries and cooldowns, explicit source attribution, payload/schema change detection, and rollback. Missing postings must not become closures unless that source's completeness and closure semantics justify it. TEST writes must target an isolated backend because current TEST/PROD share Supabase; production deployment remains user's later decision.
